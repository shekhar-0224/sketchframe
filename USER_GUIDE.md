# SketchFrame — User Guide

**Turn a script, a poem or an idea into an animated comic movie.**

You don't need to know anything about film or AI to use SketchFrame. If you can paste text and press buttons, you can make a movie.

---

## 1. Start

- **Start free.** Press **Start free — no account needed**. No signup, no card, no keys.
- **Keep your work.** Guest stories live in this browser. Press **Save my work** (the black bar at the top) and confirm with your face or fingerprint. After that, **Saved your work before? Sign in with face or fingerprint** brings you back on this device. (Only use that link once you've saved; otherwise your browser asks you to scan a phone or insert a key, because there's nothing to sign in to yet.)

## 2. Make a new story

Press **＋ New story** and choose how to begin:

| Choose | What you give it | What you get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paste script** | A screenplay or comic script | Scenes, shots and characters from your pages |
| **Upload file** | TXT, PDF, DOCX, Fountain or Final Draft (FDX) | Same as paste |
| **Type an idea** | One or two sentences | We write a short script for you first |
| **Poem / lyrics** | A poem or song | Each stanza becomes a scene, each line a narrated shot |
| **Blank board** | Nothing | An empty board you fill yourself |

Give it a title and press **Break it down**. Wait a few seconds while it reads your story; you're then taken to step 2.

> Tip: not sure what to write? Press **try a sample scene** or **try a sample poem**.

## 3. The five steps

The steps run in order and unlock one by one: **Script → Check → Cast → Board → Movie**. On a phone they sit at the bottom of the screen; on a computer they're at the top. A locked step tells you what to finish first.

### 1. Script
Your text. Change it any time, then press **Re-run breakdown** to rebuild the shots. Your characters and their pictures are kept.

### 2. Check
How we split your story: the characters we found, then every scene and shot. Fix anything that's wrong: what happens, who talks, what they say and who's in the shot. You can also delete or add shots. Nothing is drawn until you press **Looks good — draw my characters**.

### 3. Cast
Your characters are drawn first, automatically, so every panel can copy them.
- Change **What they look like** and press **Redraw sheet** if someone looks wrong.
- **Upload reference** lets you add your own drawing or photo. It's used first.
- **Lock** a character once you like them. Locked sheets are never redrawn by accident.

When everyone is drawn, you're taken to the board.

### 4. Board
Your panels. Each one has just three buttons:

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Draw / Redraw** | Draws (or draws again) this panel |
| **Edit** | Change what happens, who talks and what they say |
| **⋯** | More: *Draw on it*, *Use my own sketch*, *Replace picture*, *Copy this shot*, *Download picture*, *Delete shot* |

- The first time you open the board, every panel is drawn for you. **Draw all** at the top draws any that are still empty.
- **Drag** a panel to move it.
- **Draw on it** opens a simple pen and eraser. Save, and your marks can guide the next redraw.
- **Use my own sketch** takes a rough doodle (stick figures are fine) and turns it into a clean panel.

### 5. Movie
Watch your story come alive, in three steps:

1. **Voices.** Press **Record all voices**, then pick a voice for each character and press 🔊 to hear it. Poems are read by a narrator.
2. **Motion.** This is automatic and free. Each panel moves in 3D-like layers, and weather and light come from your words: rain, neon flicker, fog, headlights, lightning, snow. Change the camera move or switch effects on and off for any shot.
3. **AI animation (coming with paid plans).** Turns one panel into a real moving clip.

Then:
- **Export movie** saves a video file with voices and captions.
- **Get a share link** gives you a link anyone can watch, with no account needed. Press **Stop sharing** to turn it off.
- **Export PDF** (top right) makes a printable storyboard plus shot list.

## 4. Cost

**SketchFrame is free right now.** Everyone gets the good AI: sharp drawings, real character voices and the movie. There's a daily limit of drawings per person (shown under the gear key). When you reach it, drawings switch to a simpler free model until the next day.

**Paid plans are coming soon.** They'll let you connect your own AI accounts and keys.

## 5. Quick fixes

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| A face looks different in one panel | Lock the character on **Cast**, then **Redraw** that panel. For important shots, open **Edit → More options → Drawing model → Best**. |
| A panel shows an error | Press **Try again**. If it says something about billing or credits, top up that AI account. |
| Voices don't play in the exported video | Browser voices are preview-only. Pick OpenAI, Sarvam or ElevenLabs in Settings. |
| The movie looks flat | Wait for "Preparing depth…" on the Movie tab to finish; the first time downloads a small model. |
| I lost my guest stories | Guest stories live in one browser. Sign in next time to keep them everywhere. |
